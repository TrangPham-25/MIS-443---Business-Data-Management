## 📚 Course Overview – MIS 443: Business Data Management

### 📝 Course Description
This course provides an interdisciplinary approach to database management, integrating concepts from data science, information systems, and business strategy. Students gain hands-on experience through projects and case studies that simulate real-world data management challenges.

The course covers data capture, analysis, and transformation into actionable insights using **SQL** and **Python**, placing a strong emphasis on **ETL processes** to ensure effective data storage, integration, and utilization.

To foster a problem-based learning environment, students participate in class activities that involve practical, real-world scenarios.

---

### 🎯 Course Objectives
By the end of this course, students will be able to:

* **Core Concepts:** Understand and critically analyze key concepts, technologies, and methodologies related to databases, data warehouses, and decision support systems.
* **ETL Processes:** Apply data extraction, transformation, and loading (ETL) techniques across various business domains.
* **Technical Skills:** Utilize SQL, Python, and other data management tools to derive actionable insights from business data.
* **Professional Growth:** Develop effective study habits and continuously adapt to new data management techniques as ethical and responsible business professionals.
